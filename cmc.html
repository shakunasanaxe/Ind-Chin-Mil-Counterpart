Fifteen organs, and where India does the same work

In January 2016 Xi Jinping replaced the four general departments of the PLA with fifteen organs that answer directly to the Central Military Commission. The right-hand column lists them. The left-hand column shows where the same functions are carried out in India, which is usually in several places at once.

What to take from this page

01

One joint staff that commands, one that coordinates

The Joint Staff Department plans and directs operations for all services. HQ IDS in India prepares joint plans but does not command, and operational authority stays with each service chief.

02

The Political Work Department has no Indian twin

It manages the Party apparatus across the force: appointments, ideology, discipline and the Party committees. Its director was a CMC member, which shows how central the department is.

03

Procurement is centralised in China, shared in India

The Equipment Development Department sets requirements, plans research and buys. In India the same chain runs through the services, the DAC, the Acquisition Wing, DRDO, the DDP and the DGQA, with the CCS at the top.

04

Anti-corruption is a Party function in the PLA

The Discipline Inspection Commission can investigate and expel officers within the Party system. Indian vigilance is a civilian function under the CVC, and prosecution goes through the courts.

05

Mobilisation has no counterpart at scale

The National Defense Mobilization Department runs militias, reserves, military districts and conscription machinery. India's Territorial Army is a small volunteer reserve inside the Army.

06

Read the organs as roles

Each organ is described by what it does and by where India does the same work. Where a post is shown, the chart notes if its holder has been removed.
