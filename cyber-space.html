From one support force to three, and the Indian agencies beside them

In April 2024 China dissolved the Strategic Support Force, created in 2015 to pool space, cyber and information capabilities, and replaced it with three arms. The Takshashila document on the new forces reads the breakup as an acknowledgement that the combined model had not worked. India took a different route: three small tri-service agencies, one for cyber, one for space and one for special operations, working inside a joint staff.

What to take from this page

01

Status is the difference that matters most

The Cyberspace Force and Aerospace Force are theatre-grade, which puts them in the same rank as the Western Theatre Command itself. The Defence Cyber Agency and Defence Space Agency are led by two-star officers and sit below the Chief of Integrated Defence Staff.

02

The three new arms divide the old SSF unevenly

The Aerospace and Cyberspace Forces mostly took over the mandates of their predecessors, while the Information Support Force pulled together functions, including intelligence and reconnaissance, that had been scattered. That is the pattern described in the Takshashila paper.

03

India's space and cyber capacity sits partly outside the military

ISRO, CERT-In, NTRO and the NCIIPC are civilian. China's are formally civilian as well, but the military-civil fusion strategy narrows the distance between them.

04

There is no Indian Information Support Force

The closest Indian elements are the services' signals and information warfare units, the DIA, and the C4I networks of each service. Nothing combines them across the force.

05

Public knowledge is thin on both sides

No leadership or structure was announced for the Chinese Aerospace and Cyberspace Forces after their creation, and the DCyA's strength is not published. Take care with any claim about capability.

06

Doctrine will decide how this works

Whether either country gets an advantage from its structure will depend on joint doctrine, training and the way cyber and space effects are tied to the theatre commands.
