Command and control · 06

From the Chairman down to the platoon

Counting levels of command in the two systems is harder than it looks, because the PLA distinguishes between a person's rank and the grade of their post, and because it has taken the division out of its army. Select a level below to see the Chinese and Indian formations that occupy it, and what is different about them.

Corps commanders and their counterparts

A group army commander and an Indian corps commander

India · Corps commander (GOC)

Lieutenant General

Reports to
An Army Commander, who is also a lieutenant general
Commands
Divisions, plus corps troops such as artillery, engineers, signals and air defence
Chain
Single-service. Army corps commanders operate with the Air Force through coordination, and without a joint commander above them
Party presence
None
Examples
XIV Corps (Leh); IV Corps (Tezpur); XVII Mountain Strike Corps
China · Group army commander

Lieutenant General or Major General (corps-grade post)

Reports to
The theatre commander for operations, and the Army headquarters for administration and training
Commands
Combined-arms brigades and support brigades, without the division layer
Chain
Joint. A theatre commander integrates the Army with the air, naval and rocket forces under a single command
Party presence
A political commissar of equal grade, and a Party committee
Examples
76th and 77th Group Armies (Western Theatre)
In short. The post is similar, but the authority differs. An Indian corps commander leads a single-service formation and coordinates sideways. A group army commander leads a force assigned to a theatre and is commanded, in operations, by someone who is not an army officer by necessity. In addition, the Chinese commander shares leadership with a commissar, which has no Indian parallel.

Rank equivalences are approximate. The PLA separates rank (军衔) from the grade of a post (职务等级), so an officer of a given rank may hold a post of a higher or lower grade. The Senior Colonel (大校) rank has no exact Indian counterpart and is normally treated as equal to a one-star officer.
