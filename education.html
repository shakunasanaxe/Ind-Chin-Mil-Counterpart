The NDU and the NDC, and what sits around them

Professional military education is where a country's idea of its officer corps shows most clearly. The Chinese National Defense University and the Academy of Military Sciences sit directly under the Central Military Commission and teach joint command alongside the Party line. India's National Defence College is a one-year course within the Ministry of Defence, and the long-discussed national defence university has not materialised.

The education ladder

India

NDA, IMA, INA, AFA and OTA

Future officers of the three services spend three years together at the National Defence Academy at Khadakwasla, then separate to the service academies. The joint first phase is meant to build habits of working together.

China

Military academies and civilian recruitment

Officers come from PLA academies and from civilian university recruitment, with service-specific training from the start. The system has moved toward better-educated officers since 2015.

What differs. India has a joint first phase. China's officers are shaped by their own service and by the Party school system from the outset.
India

Defence Services Staff College, Wellington

A tri-service staff course for mid-career officers, with the Army War College, Naval War College and Air War College for single-service higher command.

China

Service command colleges

The Army Command College and Naval Command College in Nanjing, the Air Force Command College in Beijing and the Rocket Force Command College in Wuhan. Joint content is concentrated at the NDU.

What differs. India has one joint staff college. China teaches the staff stage by service and the joint stage later, at the NDU.
India

National Defence College, New Delhi

Founded in 1960 on the model of the Imperial Defence College. A one-year course for brigadier-level officers and civil servants, with an M.Phil. link to the University of Madras.

China

National Defense University, Beijing

Formed in 1985, restructured in 2017. A university with colleges for joint operations, strategy, politics and other fields, training officers who will lead corps and theatres.

What differs. The NDU is an institution of several colleges and degree programmes. The NDC is one course. Comparing them directly overstates how alike they are.
India

CLAWS, USI, CENJOWS, MP-IDSA and others

Think tanks funded by or affiliated with the services and the Ministry of Defence. They publish and advise, but doctrine is issued by HQ IDS and the service headquarters.

China

Academy of Military Sciences

A research academy directly under the CMC, publishing the Science of Military Strategy and shaping doctrine, with its own frontier technology institute.

What differs. The AMS develops doctrine as a standing institution of the military. In India, doctrine comes from staff directorates and think tanks play a supporting role.
India

DIAT, College of Military Engineering, MCTE

Technical education is divided between a deemed university under DRDO and service engineering colleges, with the IITs supplying much of the wider talent base.

China

NUDT and the service engineering universities

NUDT in Changsha is a military science and engineering university under the CMC, known for supercomputing and space and information systems.

What differs. NUDT is a single large institution under direct central control, whereas India distributes technical education across several bodies.

What to take from this page

01

Both are apex institutions of different kinds

The NDU trains and researches, and the NDC trains. India has no university-scale institution of this kind, which is why the proposed Indian National Defence University keeps returning to the agenda.

02

Doctrine has an owner in China

The Academy of Military Sciences does the work of writing and revising it. In India joint and service doctrine comes out of HQ IDS and the service headquarters, with think tanks in support.

03

Education and Party loyalty are bound together

The NDU is also a site of Party education, and officers' promotions depend on both military and political credentials.

04

Joint education arrives earlier in India

The NDA and the DSSC give Indian officers a tri-service experience at the start and middle of a career. Chinese officers meet other services mostly at senior levels.

05

Reform shaped the map

The 2016-17 reforms merged or closed many Chinese academies and rebuilt the system around fewer, larger institutions.

06

Check status before citing

The status of India's defence university proposal, and of the placement of the Information Engineering University under the ISF, should be checked against current official sources.
